# Level 2 · Formulas & Functions

**Level:** Beginner → Intermediate

The core function families every analyst uses daily: logic, text, dates, statistics, conditional aggregation, and lookups.

| # | Lesson | Time | Objectives |
|:-:|---|:-:|---|
| 2.1 | [Logical Functions: IF, AND, OR, IFS & More](01-logical-functions/README.md) | 105 min | • Compare values to produce TRUE/FALSE and use booleans in math<br>• Make decisions with IF, nested IF, and IFS<br>• Combine conditions with AND, OR, NOT (and XOR)<br>• Map codes to labels with SWITCH; trap errors with IFERROR and IFNA |
| 2.2 | [Text Functions](02-text-functions/README.md) | 110 min | • Extract parts of text with LEFT, RIGHT, MID, FIND, and SEARCH<br>• Clean and standardize text with TRIM, CLEAN, UPPER, LOWER, PROPER, and SUBSTITUTE<br>• Join text with &, CONCAT, and TEXTJOIN; format numbers as text with TEXT<br>• Use modern TEXTBEFORE, TEXTAFTER, and TEXTSPLIT (Microsoft 365 and Excel 2024) |
| 2.3 | [Dates & Times](03-date-time-functions/README.md) | 115 min | • Understand date serial numbers and times as fractions of a day<br>• Build and take apart dates with DATE, YEAR, MONTH, DAY, WEEKDAY, EOMONTH, and EDATE<br>• Calculate ages, lengths of stay, and turnaround times (DATEDIF, YEARFRAC, NETWORKDAYS, WORKDAY)<br>• Do time math for ED waits and overnight shifts (MOD, [h]:mm) |
| 2.4 | [Math & Statistical Functions](04-math-statistical-functions/README.md) | 100 min | • Round correctly with ROUND, ROUNDUP, ROUNDDOWN, MROUND, CEILING.MATH, and FLOOR.MATH<br>• Use INT, TRUNC, MOD, ABS, and SUMPRODUCT<br>• Describe data with MEDIAN, MODE, STDEV, PERCENTILE, and QUARTILE — and know when the mean misleads<br>• Rank and pick extremes with RANK.EQ, LARGE, and SMALL |
| 2.5 | [Conditional Counting & Summing](05-conditional-aggregation/README.md) | 110 min | • Count and sum with conditions using COUNTIF(S), SUMIF(S), and AVERAGEIF(S)<br>• Find conditional extremes with MAXIFS and MINIFS<br>• Write criteria with operators, cell references, wildcards, and date ranges<br>• Build summary grids that fill with a single formula |
| 2.6 | [Lookups: VLOOKUP, INDEX/MATCH & XLOOKUP](06-lookup-functions/README.md) | 125 min | • Look up exact matches with VLOOKUP and XLOOKUP<br>• Use approximate matches for tiers like age bands and BMI categories<br>• Combine INDEX and MATCH for flexible and two-way lookups<br>• Handle missing values with IFNA and XLOOKUP's if_not_found |
